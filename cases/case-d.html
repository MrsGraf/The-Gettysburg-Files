DEVICE REFERENCE

PARALLELISM

DEFINITION

Parallelism means that two or more phrases or clauses follow the same or a similar grammatical structure.

EXAMPLE

She wanted to learn, to grow and to succeed.

All three elements follow the same grammatical pattern: to + infinitive.

ANTITHESIS

DEFINITION

Antithesis places contrasting ideas close together to make their opposition especially clear.

antithesis /ænˈtɪθ.ə.sɪs/ · HEAR PRONUNCIATION

EXAMPLE

She wanted peace, but he chose war.

The sentence places two opposing ideas next to each other: peace ↔ war. The conjunction but makes the contrast explicit.

All three devices are present in the recovered formula:

Epiphora results from the repeated ending the people.

Rule of Three organises the complete statement into three coordinated parts.

The devices overlap. However, Rule of Three is the dominant device because it describes the overall structure of the complete formula. Parallelism and epiphora explain how this three-part structure is constructed.
